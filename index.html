Patterns

These views describe your own logged data, nothing more. An empty patch can simply mean you were busy or asleep.

Nothing here yet — once you start logging, your pattern builds up day by day. After a couple of weeks the daily rhythm shows at a glance.

Last 14 days

Each row is a day, left to right is midnight to midnight. A pink block means movement was logged in that half hour. The lighter band is your waking hours; faded areas are before you started logging, or still to come today.

Your typical day

How often each half hour had logged activity across the last 14 days — taller means more of those days.

Episodes per day

Each dot is a day's count of activity episodes; the line is the 7-day median. Markers show weeks of pregnancy.

Tag notes

About Cute Kicks

        
Cute Kicks shows your own data so you can get to know
        your baby's individual pattern of movement. It never scores, judges or
        predicts. If you think your baby's movements have slowed, stopped or changed,
        contact your midwife or maternity unit straight away — don't wait, and don't
        rely on this app.
